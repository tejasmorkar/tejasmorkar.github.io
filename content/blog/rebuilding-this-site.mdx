This site used to be a single HTML file, hand-written, no framework, styled with one stylesheet. That was fine for a few years of student projects and conference decks. It stopped being fine once the most interesting thing I do — LLM feature work — lived nowhere on it except a line in the page title.

So: a rebuild. Next.js, a real About/Experience page instead of a tagline, and this — a place to actually write things down instead of linking out to five-year-old Medium posts.

The plan isn't to blog on a schedule. It's to have the infrastructure ready for whenever there's something worth writing — a RAG failure mode, an agentic system that didn't do what I expected, a note on shipping ML into a product people actually use. More on that as it happens.
