This site used to be a single HTML file with one stylesheet and no framework at all. I built it back in college and it did its job for years. It had my student projects, the sessions I took, and links to my Medium articles.

But the work I do now, building LLM features, was nowhere on it except one line under my name. So, I rebuilt it with Next.js.

Now there's a proper About and Experience page, and this Writing section. All my old Medium articles live here too, so you don't have to jump to another site to read them.

I'm not going to post on a schedule. Whenever I have something worth sharing, like a RAG setup that broke in a weird way or an agent that did something I didn't expect, it'll go here.

If you have any feedback about the site, feel free to reach out through the [Contact](/contact) page!
